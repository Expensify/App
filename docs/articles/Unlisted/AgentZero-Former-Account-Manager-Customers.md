# AgentZero Reference: Customers Who Previously Had an Account Manager

Use this reference when a customer:

- Says they previously had a dedicated Account Manager but no longer do.
- Asks what happened to their Account Manager.
- Asks whether they can get their Account Manager back.
- Wants to continue working with their previous Account Manager.
- Says moving from an Account Manager to the Support Desk feels like a downgrade.
- Wants to know whether they can work with the same support person again.
- Wants to know whether human support is available by chat or call.

## What changed for customers who previously had an account manager

Expensify has expanded how customers reach human support:

- **Concierge is 100% AI.** It answers instantly, 24/7, for everything it supports. It still points customers to a human whenever they need one, or whenever they ask.
- **The Support Desk is 100% human.** When a customer selects **Talk to a human**, a support rep opens a dedicated support ticket with them. That rep owns the ticket from start to finish, so there are no handoffs between messages.
- **The Support Desk is staffed by experienced people.** Many Support Desk reps have worked as Expensify account managers. Customers are getting the same expertise, but without waiting for one person's calendar.
- **Customers can get help by chat or on a call.** A rep can set up a call when it would help solve the problem faster.
- **Customers are never stuck with one rep.** If a ticket isn't going the way they want, they can select **Talk to a human** again to get someone new.

## What to tell customers who previously had an account manager

Lead with what the customer gains. Keep the tone upbeat, warm, and confident.

1. **Faster help, any time.** Customers no longer need to wait for one person to be available. They can reach a human right away, whenever they need one.
2. **The same expertise.** The people on the Support Desk include experienced former account managers. This is not a step down in knowledge.
3. **Chat or call, their choice.** Customers can solve things in chat or get on a call with a rep.
4. **One owner per ticket.** The rep who picks up the ticket sees it through, so the customer never has to repeat themselves.
5. **Continuity is coming.** In the coming weeks, customers will be able to choose to talk to their previous support person again when they select **Talk to a human**. If they value working with the same person, they can choose that option in the future once the **Talk to your previous support person** option is available.

## How to respond when a customer asks about losing their account manager

- Acknowledge the change first. Then explain the benefits that are most relevant to their question.
- Tell the customer how to reach a human now: select **Talk to a human**.
- If the customer cares about continuity, mention that the option to talk to their previous support person again is coming in the coming weeks.
- Keep it short. One or two benefits is usually enough.

## What not to promise to customers about account managers and the support desk

- Do not speculate about why the customer's Account Manager access changed, or say the change was made to cut costs, reduce service, or downgrade the customer.
- Do not promise to restore a dedicated account manager.
- Do not promise a specific person will be available at a specific time.
- Do not give a date for the **Talk to your previous support person** option. Say **in the coming weeks**.
- Do not name or share contact details for a former account manager.

## Example responses for customers who previously had an account manager

### “Where did my account manager go?” / “Why don't I have an account manager anymore?”

> Great question! We've expanded how you can reach our team. Instead of relying on one person's availability, you can now select **Talk to a human** any time to open a support ticket with our Support Desk. The team includes experienced former account managers, and the rep who picks up your ticket owns it from start to finish. You can chat or hop on a call, whatever works best for you.

### “I want my account manager back.” / “I liked working with one person.”

> Totally understand. Working with someone who knows your setup makes a big difference! Good news: in the coming weeks, when you select **Talk to a human**, you'll be able to choose to talk to your previous support person again. You can also DM your previous Account Manager and that will generate a ticket that will get assigned to them immediately. In the meantime, select **Talk to a human** any time and an experienced rep will pick up your ticket and see it through.

### “This feels like a downgrade.”

> I hear you, and we've designed this to be an upgrade. You no longer have to wait for one person to be free. You can reach an experienced human, many of whom have been account managers themselves, whenever you need one, by chat or on a call. And if a ticket isn't heading where you want, select **Talk to a human** again to get someone new.

### “Can I get on a call with someone?”

> Absolutely! Select **Talk to a human** to open a support ticket. Your rep can help you right there in chat or set up a call if that's easier.

### “Will I have to explain everything again every time?”

> Within a ticket, no. The rep who picks it up owns it from start to finish, so there's no passing you between people. And in the coming weeks, you'll be able to choose to talk to your previous support person again, so you can keep working with someone who already knows your account.

## What AgentZero should know about Concierge, the support desk, and account managers

- Concierge is AI-only. Human support happens through **Talk to a human** in a dedicated support ticket.
- Customers who still have an account manager can keep messaging them as they do today.
- **Talk to your previous support person** is not currently available, but is a planned update in the coming weeks.
