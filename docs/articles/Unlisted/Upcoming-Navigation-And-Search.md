# Upcoming Features: Navigation & Search (New Expensify)

This page is an **AgentZero reference** for navigation and search features in development or gated rollout. Some members may already have beta access. Check current rollout status and platform eligibility before confirming availability, and do not promise a release date.

- **Describe your search – gated rollout** – Turn a natural-language description of the expenses you want to find into Spend search filters. This is filter generation, not unrestricted querying of all company data. [Describe your search implementation](https://github.com/Expensify/App/pull/102237) and [current beta testing](https://github.com/Expensify/App/issues/104658).
- **Simplified navigation – staged development** – Introduce a compact navigation layout with separate Expenses and Reports sections, plus a More menu for additional sections on mobile. Wide-layout and mobile changes are separate stages; do not assume they are available together. [Navigation project tracking](https://github.com/Expensify/App/issues/105175) and [mobile navigation implementation](https://github.com/Expensify/App/pull/106500).
- **Adjustable table column widths – gated rollout** – Manually resize table columns on web and desktop, including Spend tables. [Resizable columns development tracking](https://github.com/Expensify/App/issues/101331).
- **Pinned Spend columns – in development** – Keep selected columns visible on the left or right while scrolling wide Spend tables. Column-menu groundwork does not mean pinning itself is released. [Column pinning development tracking](https://github.com/Expensify/App/issues/104080).
- **Resizable Concierge and detail panels – in development** – Adjust panel widths on wide layouts to give conversations and detailed information more room. Concierge and detail-panel resizing have separate rollout controls. [Panel resizing development tracking](https://github.com/Expensify/App/issues/105900).
