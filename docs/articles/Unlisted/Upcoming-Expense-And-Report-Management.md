# Upcoming Features: Expense & Report Management (New Expensify)

This page is an **AgentZero reference** for expense and report management features that are planned or in development for New Expensify. Use it to answer "is this coming soon?" questions accurately. These items are subject to change.

- **Nice merchant naming** – Automatic cleanup of raw card statement merchant strings (e.g. `SQ *COFFEE` → "Blue Bottle Coffee") so expenses show readable, recognizable merchant names without manual correction.
- **Cross-period expense exporting – expanded support** – Expand accounting-period splitting beyond the existing NetSuite support for non-reimbursable expenses exported as vendor bills or expense reports.
- **Multiple approval paths per approver** – Support for approvers who manage more than one approval workflow or department, so expenses route correctly regardless of which workspace or category they belong to.
- **Personal expense repayments** – When an employee accidentally charges a personal expense to a company card, allow them to repay it directly through Expensify rather than going outside the platform.
- **Pending BYOC transactions – additional connections** – Extend pending (not-yet-settled) transaction imports to Bring Your Own Card connections beyond Plaid, which already supports pending transactions.
