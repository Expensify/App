# Instacart

Connect Instacart Business with Expensify to automatically forward receipts for eligible business orders to Expensify. An Instacart Business account owner can connect Expensify for the organization, and receipt forwarding is automatically enabled for everyone on the team.

Receipt forwarding applies only to eligible future orders. Past receipts and orders paid by invoice aren't forwarded to Expensify.

## Who can connect Instacart Business to Expensify

To set up receipt forwarding for Instacart Business, you must:

- Have an Expensify account.
- Be an account owner in Instacart Business.

## How to connect Instacart Business to Expensify

Follow Instacart's instructions to connect Expensify as an expense system: [Add an expense system](https://comparefoodsnc.instacart.com/help/section/3375565582/2218464804).

## What happens after you connect Instacart Business to Expensify

Once connected, receipt forwarding is automatically enabled for you and everyone on your team. Eligible future Instacart Business receipts are automatically forwarded to Expensify.

## How to turn off Instacart receipt forwarding

You can turn off receipt forwarding for your own account, or an Instacart Business account owner can disable it for the entire organization.

- To stop forwarding your own receipts, follow Instacart's instructions to [turn off receipt forwarding for your account](https://comparefoodsnc.instacart.com/help/section/3375565582/2218464804#Turn-off-receipt-forwarding-for-your-account).
- To stop forwarding receipts for the entire organization, follow Instacart's instructions to [disable receipt forwarding for your organization](https://comparefoodsnc.instacart.com/help/section/3375565582/2218464804#Disable-receipt-forwarding-for-your-organization).

After receipt forwarding is turned off, new eligible receipts are no longer forwarded to Expensify.

# FAQ

## Do past Instacart receipts get forwarded to Expensify?

No. Receipt forwarding applies only to future eligible orders after Expensify is connected.

## Does receipt forwarding turn on automatically for team members?

Yes. When an account owner connects Expensify, receipt forwarding is automatically enabled for everyone on the team. Individual team members can follow Instacart's instructions to [turn off receipt forwarding for their account](https://comparefoodsnc.instacart.com/help/section/3375565582/2218464804#Turn-off-receipt-forwarding-for-your-account).

## Do orders paid by invoice get forwarded to Expensify?

No. Instacart Business orders paid by invoice aren't forwarded to Expensify.
