# DoorDash

Connect your DoorDash Business Profile to Expensify to automatically send eligible receipts to your Expensify account.

Once connected, receipts from eligible orders placed using your Business Profile are automatically sent to Expensify, so you don't have to add them manually.

## Who can connect DoorDash to Expensify

To connect DoorDash to Expensify, you must:

- Have a DoorDash Business Profile.
- Have an Expensify account.

## How to connect DoorDash to Expensify

Follow DoorDash's instructions to [link Expensify as your expense provider](https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers/article/setting-your-business-profile-for-easier-expensing#:~:text=For%20Emburse%2C%20Expensify%2C%20Ramp%2C%20%26%20Brex).

## What happens after you connect DoorDash to Expensify

Once connected, eligible receipts from orders placed using your DoorDash Business Profile are automatically sent to Expensify.

You can also manually send past receipts to Expensify from **Orders** in your DoorDash account.

# FAQ

## Do personal DoorDash orders sync to Expensify?

No. The Expensify connection is associated with your DoorDash Business Profile. Only eligible orders placed using your Business Profile are automatically sent to Expensify.

## Do past DoorDash receipts get sent to Expensify automatically?

No. Only eligible orders placed after Expensify is connected are automatically sent to Expensify. You can manually send past receipts from **Orders** in your DoorDash account.
