# Merge Accounts

If you have more than one Expensify account—like a personal and a company account—you can merge them. This brings all your reports, receipts, imported cards, Copilots, and workspace access into one login.

**Important:** Merging accounts is permanent and cannot be undone.

---

## Who can merge Expensify accounts

Before you start, make sure you know:

- Which email address you want to keep as your login going forward.
- Which email address you want to discard and merge in.

You must log in to the account you want to **keep** and merge the account you no longer need **into** it.

You **cannot** merge:

- A company account into a personal account.
- Two company accounts on private domains.

**Note:** If your company uses **SAML authentication**, a domain admin must approve the request. In that case, you’ll be prompted to switch to Expensify Classic to start the merge.

---

## How to merge accounts

1. Log in to the account you want to keep as your primary account.
2. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, and at the bottom on mobile), go to **Account > Security**.
3. Click **Merge accounts**.
4. Enter the **email address or phone number** of the account you want to merge into this one.
5. Check the box that says **“I understand this is not reversible.”**
6. Click **Merge accounts**.
7. Check the inbox of the account you’re merging in for the security code sent by Expensify.
8. Enter the code into the prompt and click **Merge**.

# FAQ

## What happens to my data when I merge accounts?

The following items from the merged account are transferred to your primary account:

- Receipts and expenses
- Expense reports
- Invoices and bills
- Imported cards
- Contact methods
- Copilots
- Group workspace settings

## Can I merge accounts on the mobile app?

Yes. Merging accounts works on web, desktop, and mobile. On mobile, the navigation tabs are at the bottom of the screen.

## Can a Copilot merge accounts on my behalf?

Yes, a Copilot with Full Access can merge accounts while acting on your behalf.

- When they do, Expensify sends the security code to you (the account owner), not the Copilot. You’ll need to share that code with your Copilot so they can complete the merge.
- This keeps you in control, since the merge can’t be finalized without the code that only you receive.
