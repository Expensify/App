# Archive a Workspace

If you no longer need a workspace, the workspace owner can archive it. Archiving replaces deleting a workspace. An archived workspace shows **(Archived)** after its name and becomes read-only. Nothing is permanently deleted, and the workspace owner can unarchive it.

---

## Who can archive a workspace

Only the workspace owner can archive a workspace. You can archive a workspace at any time, on any plan, including a workspace on an annual subscription.

---

## How to archive a workspace in Expensify Classic

1. Select **Settings > Workspaces**.
2. Select the cog icon next to the workspace you want to archive.
3. Select **Archive**.
4. Confirm that you want to archive the workspace.

If the workspace has company cards, archiving unassigns all cards from members and permanently deletes any unsubmitted card expenses. If the workspace has Expensify Cards, archiving sets all Expensify Card limits to $0 and declines any new purchase attempts.

---

## What happens after you archive a workspace

After you archive a workspace:

- **(Archived)** is added to the workspace name.
- The **Chat with admins** option is hidden.
- The workspace name stays a link. Selecting it takes workspace admins and owners to the workspace **Overview** page in New Expensify, in read-only mode. You can view every setting, but you can't change anything.
- On the **Reports** and **Expenses** pages, the workspace name shows **(Archived)** instead of "Not shared."
- Archived workspaces still appear in workspace filters, so you can find their reports and expenses.
- Report visibility doesn't change. Members who could see a report before the workspace was archived can still see it.
- You can't set an archived workspace as your default workspace.

---

## How archiving a workspace affects billing

Archiving a workspace doesn't switch billing off. Expensify bills for report activity, not for having an active workspace. Archiving blocks most activity, but members can still export reports from an archived workspace, and an export counts as billable activity.

- **Pay-per-use subscription:** You're billed only for members who take billable activity that month. Activity from earlier in the month, before you archived the workspace, is still billed.
- **Collect plan (billed per unique member):** Members of an archived workspace aren't billed unless they take a report action on it, such as exporting a report.
- **Annual subscription:** The commitment continues through the end of the term, even if every workspace is archived. Archiving a workspace doesn't trigger a refund. [Learn how to request a refund on an annual subscription](/articles/expensify-classic/expensify-billing/Change-Plan-Or-Subscription#annual-subscription).

---

## How to unarchive a workspace

Only the workspace owner sees the **Unarchive** option.

1. Select **Settings > Workspaces**.
2. Select the cog icon next to the archived workspace.
3. Select **Unarchive**.

Expensify opens the **Workspaces** page in New Expensify with archived workspaces already filtered, so you can find and unarchive the workspace there. [Learn how to unarchive a workspace in New Expensify](/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Archive-a-Workspace#how-to-unarchive-a-workspace).

# FAQ

## Can I delete a workspace instead of archiving it?

No. Archiving replaces deleting a workspace. Your workspace data is kept so you can unarchive the workspace later.

## Does archiving a workspace stop my billing?

Not completely. Archiving blocks most activity, but exporting a report from an archived workspace is billable activity, and an annual subscription continues through the end of the term.

## Why can't I find the Chat with admins option for my workspace?

The **Chat with admins** option is hidden for archived workspaces.
