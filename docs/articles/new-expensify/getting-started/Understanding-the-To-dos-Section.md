# Understanding the To-dos Section

The **To-dos** section shows items that currently need your attention. Each item links you to the report or view where you can take action.

**To-dos** helps you see what needs attention at a glance, without having to search through Inbox or Spend.

This section was previously called **For you**.

---

## Who sees the To-dos section

The **To-dos** section appears on Home for all members.

What you see depends on:
- Your role, such as submitter, approver, or Workspace Admin
- Whether there are reports waiting on an action from you

If nothing requires your attention, the section may appear empty.

---

## What qualifies as a to-do in the To-dos section

An item appears in **To-dos** when a report can’t move forward without something from you.

Only actions that are required to move a report along will appear here.

---

## Which items can appear in the To-dos section

The **To-dos** section can show items like: 

- **Review** – expenses on your draft reports that are flagged against your workspace’s rules and need to be reviewed before the report can be submitted
- **Submit** – reports that are ready for you to submit
- **Approve** – reports waiting for your approval
- **Pay** – approved reports waiting to be paid
- **Export** – reports waiting to be exported
- **Fix** – reports that need attention before they can move forward

---

## How To-dos is ordered against Time sensitive on Home

**To-dos** and **Time sensitive** are two separate sections on the same Home card, each with its own heading.

- **Time sensitive** always appears above **To-dos**.
- Within each section, items that need urgent attention appear above items that are simply ready for you to act on.

To learn what belongs in the other section, see [Learn what appears on Home](/articles/new-expensify/getting-started/Expensify-Home-Overview).

## How roles affect what you see in the To-dos section

The items you see in **To-dos** depend on what actions you’re responsible for.

- **Submitters** may see:
  - Review
  - Submit

- **Approvers** may see:
  - Approve

- **Workspace Admins** may see:
  - Pay
  - Export
  - Fix

---

## Where each To-dos item takes you

When you select an item in **To-dos**, you’re taken directly to where you can take action.

For example:
- **Review** takes you to the first flagged expense so you can resolve the rule violation
- **Submit** takes you to reports ready to submit
- **Approve** takes you to reports waiting for approval
- **Pay** takes you to approved reports waiting to be paid
- **Export** takes you to reports waiting to be exported
- **Fix** takes you to reports that need attention

---

## What it means when the To-dos section is empty

If there are no reports waiting on you, the **To-dos** section may be empty. This means you’re caught up and there’s nothing you need to act on right now.

The caught-up message only appears when **both** **To-dos** and **Time sensitive** are empty. If you have any **Time sensitive** item, that item is shown instead of the caught-up message.

# FAQ

## Why do I see more than one item in the To-dos section?

If you’re responsible for more than one type of action, such as approving and paying reports, you may see multiple items at the same time.

## Why don’t I see an item even though I expect one?

An item only appears when a report is waiting specifically on you. If the report is still in progress or waiting on someone else, it won’t appear in **To-dos**.

## Does the To-dos section replace Inbox or Spend?

No. **To-dos** helps you spot what needs attention and takes you to the right place. All work is still completed in Spend.

## Why don’t all my reports appear in the To-dos section?

The **To-dos** section only shows items that currently need your attention. Completed work or items that are waiting on someone else will not show in your **To-dos** section.

## What happened to the For you section?

Nothing changed about what the section does — it was renamed to **To-dos**. The items that appeared under **For you** now appear under **To-dos**.
