# Understand Workspace Member Roles

Workspace member roles determine what each member can see and do within a workspace. Assigning the appropriate role helps ensure members have the access they need while limiting access to workspace settings and administrative features.

The roles available depend on your workspace plan. Learn more about Workspace Plan Restrictions.

---

## What does each workspace member role do?

Each workspace member is assigned one role.

| Role | What it can do |
|------|----------------|
| **Member** | Submit expenses and participate in approval workflows based on the workspace configuration. |
| **Workspace admin** | Manage workspace settings, members, workflows, and other workspace configuration. |
| **People admin** | Manage workspace members and approval workflows. Can only add, remove, and update Members and Auditors. |
| **Card admin** | Manage Expensify Cards and Company Cards, including issuing cards, assigning cards, and updating spending limits. |
| **Payments admin** | Manage payment settings, including business bank accounts, payment methods, and the authorized expense payer. |
| **Auditor** | View workspace data and comment on reports with read-only access to workspace settings. |
| **Editor** | Edit workspace settings without full administrative access to the workspace. |

## How do workspace plans affect available roles?

Not every workspace plan supports every role.

For example:

- Collect workspaces support the **Workspace admin** and **Member** roles.
- Control workspaces support additional administrator roles, including **People admin**, **Card admin**, **Payments admin**, and **Auditor**.
- Submit workspaces include the **Editor** role.

Learn more about Workspace Plan Restrictions.

---

## Why should you assign different workspace roles?

Assign members the role that provides the minimum level of access they need.

For example:

- Assign **Member** to employees who only need to submit expenses.
- Assign **People admin** to someone who manages workspace membership and approval workflows.
- Assign **Payments admin** to someone responsible for reimbursement and payment settings.
- Assign **Card admin** to someone who manages company cards.
- Assign **Workspace admin** to someone who needs full administrative access.

Using the appropriate role helps protect workspace settings while giving members the permissions they need to perform their work.
