# Managing Workspace Members

Workspace members are the people who can access and submit expenses on an Expensify workspace. Each member is assigned a role that determines what they can see and what actions they can take, such as submitting expenses, approving reports, or managing workspace settings.

Workspace membership changes over time as people join, leave, or take on new responsibilities. Workspace admins and People admins can invite members, update their roles, and remove members as needed.

---

## What are workspace member roles

Every workspace member has exactly one assigned role, and that role determines the actions they can perform within the workspace. 

Member roles are assigned separately for each workspace. If someone belongs to multiple workspaces, they can have a different role in each one.

The summary below describes each role at a high level. For a complete description of each role and its permissions, see [Understand Workspace Member Roles](/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/manage-workspace-members/Understand-Workspace-Member-Roles.md).

Depending on your workspace plan, available roles include:

- Member
- Workspace admin
- People admin
- Payments admin
- Card admin
- Auditor
- Editor

## Who can manage workspace members

Workspace admins can manage all workspace members.

People admins can manage workspace membership and approval workflows, but they can only add, remove, or update Members and Auditors. They can't manage Workspace admins, Card admins, Payments admins, or other People admins.

## How do you manage workspace members?

Managing workspace members can include adding new workspace members, updating their roles, and removing members who no longer need access.

# FAQ

## Can a workspace have more than one Workspace admin?

Yes. A workspace can have multiple Workspace admins. 

## Can a member have more than one role in a workspace? 

No. Each workspace member is assigned one role at a time. However, note that member roles are assigned separately for each workspace. If someone belongs to multiple workspaces, they can have a different role in each one.

## How do I change the workspace owner?

Workspace owner isn't a member role. The workspace owner is the Workspace admin who is responsible for the workspace's billing and subscription.

To make another Workspace admin the owner, see [Transfer Workspace Ownership](/articles/new-expensify/billing-and-subscriptions/manage-your-subscription-and-billing/manage-billing/Transfer-Workspace-Ownership#how-to-transfer-workspace-ownership).

To learn more about the difference between a Workspace admin and the workspace owner, see [What Member Terms Mean](/articles/new-expensify/billing-and-subscriptions/Learn-About-Billing-Terms-and-Definitions#what-member-terms-mean).
