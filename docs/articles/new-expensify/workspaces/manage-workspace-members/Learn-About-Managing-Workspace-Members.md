Workspace members are the people who can access and submit expenses on an Expensify workspace. Each member is assigned a role that determines what they can see and what actions they can take, such as submitting expenses, approving reports, or managing workspace settings.

As your organization changes, you can invite new members, update roles, remove members, or transfer workspace ownership. Each of these tasks has its own workflow.

## What are workspace member roles

Every workspace member is assigned a role. Roles define the level of access a member has within the workspace and include: 

 - **Member**: can submit and approve reports.
 - **Payments admin:** can submit and approve reports, and also manage workflow payments.
 - **People admin**: can submit and approve reports, and also manage members and approval workflows.
 - **Payments admin:** can submit and approve reports, and also manage workflow payments.
 - **Card admin:** can submit and approve reports, and also manage workspace cards.
 - **Auditor**: can submit and approve reports, and also view and comment on all workspace reports.
 - **Workspace admin**: can manage reports and workspace settings.

## Who can manage workspace members

Workspace admins can manage all workspace members.

People admins can manage workspace membership and approval workflows, but they can only add, remove, or update Members and Auditors. They can't manage Workspace admins or other scoped administrator roles.

## How do you manage workspace members

Common member management tasks include:

 - Inviting new workspace members
 - Changing a member's role on the workspace
 - Removing workspace members
