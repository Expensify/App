# Manage Workspace Member Roles

Workspace member roles determine what members can see and do within a workspace. Assign the appropriate role based on the level of access each person needs.

The roles available depend on your workspace plan. Learn more about Workspace Plan Restrictions.

---

## Who can manage workspace member roles?

Workspace admins can assign and change all workspace member roles.

People admins can change members between the **Member** and **Auditor** roles only. They can't assign or remove the **Workspace admin** role or other scoped administrator roles.

To make someone the workspace owner, learn how to Transfer Workspace Ownership.

---

## What does each workspace member role do?

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Member** | Can submit and approve expenses based on the workspace's approval workflow. |
| **Workspace admin** | Can manage workspace settings, members, roles, workflows, and other workspace configuration. |
| **Card admin** | Can manage Expensify Cards and Company Cards, including issuing cards, setting limits, and assigning company cards. Available on Control workspaces. |
| **People admin** | Can manage workspace members and approval workflows. Can only add, remove, and update Members and Auditors. Available on Control workspaces. |
| **Payments admin** | Can manage payment settings, including business bank accounts, payment methods, and the authorized expense payer. Available on Control workspaces. |
| **Auditor** | Has read-only access to workspace settings and can view and comment on reports. Available on Control workspaces. |
| **Editor** | Can edit workspace settings but can't manage member roles or view all workspace expenses. Available on Submit workspaces. |

## How to change a workspace member's role

1. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), select **Workspaces > [Workspace name]**.
2. Select **Members**.
3. Select the member whose role you want to change.
4. Select **Role**.
5. Choose the new workspace role.
6. Save your changes.

---

## What happens after you change a workspace member's role?

The member's permissions are updated immediately based on their new role.

Their new permissions apply the next time they access the workspace.

# FAQ

## Which workspace role should I assign?

Assign the role that provides the minimum level of access the member needs to perform their work.

## Why can't I assign a specific role?

The available roles depend on your workspace plan and your own permissions. For example, People admins can only assign the **Member** and **Auditor** roles.

## Can I make another member the workspace owner?

Yes. Workspace ownership is transferred separately from changing a member's role. Learn how to Transfer Workspace Ownership.

## Can I change multiple members' roles at once?

No. Change each member's role individually from the **Members** page.
