# Invite Workspace Members

nvite people to your workspace so they can submit expenses, approve reports, or manage workspace settings based on the role you assign them.

There are several ways to add members to a workspace:

Invite members individually using their name, email address, or phone number.
Import members from a spreadsheet.
Sync members from a supported HR or accounting integration.

This article explains how to invite members individually.

---

## Who can invite workspace members?

Workspace admins can invite members to any workspace.

People admins can invite members, but they can only assign the **Member** and **Auditor** roles.

The roles available when inviting a member depend on your workspace plan. Collect workspaces support the **Workspace admin** and **Member** roles only. Control workspaces support all roles. 

If you're unsure which role to assign, learn about Workspace Member Roles.

---

## How to invite workspace members

1. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), select **Workspaces > [workspace name]**.
2. Select **Members**.
3. Select **Invite member**.
4. Search for the person by name, email address, or phone number, then select them from the list.
5. Click **Next**.
6. Select the workspace role you want the member to have.
7. Select the approver who should review the member's submitted expenses.
8. (Optional) Enter a message to include with the invitation.
9. Click **Invite**.

## What happens after you invite a workspace member?

The invited person is added to the workspace immediately with the role you selected.

If they already have an Expensify account, the workspace appears in their account automatically and they'll receive an email notification.

If they don't already have an Expensify account, they'll receive an email inviting them to create an account and join the workspace.

# FAQ

## Why can't I invite someone to my workspace?

You must be a Workspace admin or People admin to invite members. Also verify that the person's email address or phone number is entered correctly.

## Can I invite multiple members at the same time?

Yes. To add many members at once, you can:

 - Import members from a spreadsheet.
 - Sync members from a supported HR or accounting integration.

## Why can't I assign certain roles?

The roles you can assign depend on your workspace plan and your own workspace role. For example, People admins can only assign the Member and Auditor roles.

Learn more in Manage Workspace Member Roles and Understand Workspace Plan Restrictions.
