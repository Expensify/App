# Remove Workspace Members

Remove members from your workspace when they no longer need access. You can remove members individually or remove multiple members at the same time.

---

## Who can remove workspace members?

Workspace admins can remove any workspace member.

People admins can remove members with the **Member** and **Auditor** roles only. They can't remove Workspace admins or other scoped administrator roles.

---

## How to remove a workspace member

1. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), select **Workspaces > [Workspace name]**.
2. Select **Members**.
3. Select the member you want to remove.
4. Select **Remove from workspace**.
5. Select **Remove** to confirm.

## How to remove multiple workspace members

1. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), select **Workspaces > [Workspace name]**.
2. Select **Members**.
3. Select the checkbox next to each member you want to remove.
4. Select the bulk actions menu.
5. Select **Remove member(s)**.
6. Select **Remove** to confirm.

## What happens after you remove a workspace member?

The member immediately loses access to the workspace.

Removing a member doesn't delete their Expensify account. It only removes their access to the selected workspace.

# FAQ

## Why can't I remove a workspace member?

You must be a Workspace admin or People admin to remove workspace members.

People admins can only remove members with the **Member** and **Auditor** roles.

## Can I remove multiple members at the same time?

Yes. Select multiple members from the **Members** page, then use the bulk removal option.

## Does removing a member delete their Expensify account?

No. Removing a member only removes their access to your workspace.
