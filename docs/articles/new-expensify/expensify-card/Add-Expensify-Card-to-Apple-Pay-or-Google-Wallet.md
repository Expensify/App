# Add the Expensify Card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet

You can use your Expensify Card for contactless in-person payments by adding it to Apple Pay or Google Wallet.

---

## Who can add an Expensify Card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet

Any member with an active Expensify Card can add it to a supported mobile wallet.

---

## How to add your Expensify Card to Apple Pay (iOS)

Follow Apple's guide to set up Apple Pay: [How to add a debit or credit card on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108398)

---

## How to add your Expensify Card to Google Wallet (Android)

Follow Google's guide to set up Google Wallet: [Add a debit or credit card to the Google Wallet app](https://support.google.com/wallet/answer/12058983)

---

## How to add your Expensify Card from the Expensify mobile app

If you can't add your card directly from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, you can add it from the Expensify mobile app instead.

1. Open the Expensify mobile app.
2. Go to **Account** > **Wallet**.
3. Select your card.
4. Tap **Add to Apple Wallet** or **Add to Google Wallet**.

---

## How to approve a pending Expensify Card addition to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet

Some Expensify Card wallet additions need your approval before the card works in that wallet. When approval is required, a row appears in the **Time sensitive** section of your Home page. This row appears on web and mobile, so you can approve the request from any platform.

1. Go to **Home** in the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile).
2. In the **Time sensitive** section, find the row that reads Apple Wallet card addition needs your approval or Google Wallet card addition needs your approval.
3. Select **Review**.
4. On the **Confirm your request** screen, check the wallet name and the last four digits of the card.
5. Select **Confirm** to approve the request, or **Deny** to reject it.
6. If you selected **Confirm**, enter the security code Expensify sends to your primary contact method.
7. Select **Got it** to close the confirmation screen.

## What happens after you approve or deny an Expensify Card wallet addition

If you select **Confirm** and enter the correct security code, Expensify shows **Success!** and your card becomes active in that wallet. The row disappears from the **Time sensitive** section.

If you select **Deny**, Expensify shows **Request denied** and does not add the card to that wallet. No security code is required to deny a request, and the row disappears from the **Time sensitive** section. To add the card later, start the wallet addition again.

---

## What happens after you add your Expensify Card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet

After your card is successfully added and any approval request is confirmed, you can use Apple Pay or Google Wallet anywhere contactless payments are accepted. Purchases made with your mobile wallet appear on the same Expensify Card account as purchases made with your physical card.

# FAQ

## Why do I see the error "This card cannot be used"?

If you see the **This card cannot be used** message while trying to add your card to Apple Wallet, add the card from within the Expensify mobile app instead.

## Can I add my Expensify Card from the Expensify web app?

No. You can't add your Expensify Card to a wallet while using Expensify in a web browser. Use the Expensify mobile app or add your card directly from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet instead. You can still approve or deny a pending wallet addition request from the **Time sensitive** section of your Home page on web.

## Why don't I see the wallet approval request on my Home page?

The row only appears while a wallet addition is waiting on you. It disappears as soon as you select **Confirm** or **Deny**, or once the request is resolved another way. If the row is gone and your card doesn't work in your wallet, add the card to your wallet again.

## Which contact method receives the security code?

Expensify sends the security code to your primary contact method. The **Let's verify it's you** screen shows which contact method it was sent to.
