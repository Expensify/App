# Add the Expensify Card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet

You can use your Expensify Card for contactless in-person payments by adding it to Apple Pay or Google Wallet.

---

## Who can add an Expensify Card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet

Any member with an active Expensify Card can add it to a supported mobile wallet.

---

## How to add your Expensify Card to Apple Pay (iOS)

Follow Apple's guide to set up Apple Pay: [How to add a debit or credit card on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108398)

---

## How to add your Expensify Card to Google Wallet (Android)

Follow Google's guide to set up Google Wallet: [Add a debit or credit card to the Google Wallet app](https://support.google.com/wallet/answer/12058983)

---

## How to add your Expensify Card from the Expensify mobile app

If you can't add your card directly from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, you can add it from the Expensify mobile app instead.

1. Open the Expensify mobile app.
2. Go to **Account** > **Wallet**.
3. Select your card.
4. Tap **Add to Apple Wallet** or **Add to Google Wallet**.

---

## Where to find an Expensify Card wallet addition that needs your approval

Some wallet additions need your approval before the card works in that wallet. When approval is required, Expensify shows the message **Apple Wallet card addition needs your approval** or **Google Wallet card addition needs your approval**, each with a **Review** button. You can approve from web or mobile.

The message appears in three places:

- On **Home**, in the **Time sensitive** section.
- On the **Wallet** page, below the card in the **Cards** section.
- On the card details page, below the card image.

A green dot also appears next to **Wallet** in the **Account** menu while a request is waiting on you.

## How to approve or deny an Expensify Card addition to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet

1. Go to **Home** in the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), or go to **Account** > **Wallet**.
2. Find the approval message and select **Review**.
3. On the **Confirm your request** screen, check the wallet name and the last four digits of the card.
4. Select **Confirm** to approve the request, or **Deny** to reject it.
5. If you selected **Confirm**, enter the security code Expensify sends to your primary contact method on the **Let's verify it's you** screen.
6. Select **Got it** to close the confirmation screen.

---

## What happens after you approve or deny an Expensify Card wallet addition

If you select **Confirm** and enter the correct security code, Expensify shows **Success!** and your card becomes active in that wallet.

If you select **Deny**, Expensify shows **Request denied** and does not add the card to that wallet. Denying does not require a security code. To add the card later, start the wallet addition again.

Either way, the approval message and the green dot disappear from **Home**, the **Wallet** page, the card details page, and the **Account** menu.

---

## What happens after you add your Expensify Card to Apple Pay or Google Wallet

After your card is successfully added, and after you confirm any request that needs your approval, you can use Apple Pay or Google Wallet anywhere contactless payments are accepted. Purchases made with your mobile wallet appear on the same Expensify Card account as purchases made with your physical card.

# FAQ

## Why do I see the error "This card cannot be used"?

If you see the **This card cannot be used** message while trying to add your card to Apple Wallet, add the card from within the Expensify mobile app instead.

## Can I add my Expensify Card from the Expensify web app?

No. You can't add your Expensify Card to a wallet while using Expensify in a web browser. Use the Expensify mobile app or add your card directly from Apple Wallet or Google Wallet instead. You can still approve or deny a wallet addition that needs your approval from a web browser.

## Why can't I find the message saying my card addition needs my approval?

The message only appears while a wallet addition is waiting on you. It disappears as soon as you select **Confirm** or **Deny**, or once the request is resolved another way. If the message is gone and your card still doesn't work in your wallet, add the card to your wallet again.

## Which contact method receives the security code?

Expensify sends the security code to your primary contact method. The **Let's verify it's you** screen names the contact method it was sent to.

## Why does the message say Digital wallet instead of Apple Wallet or Google Wallet?

Expensify names the wallet whenever the request identifies it. When the request doesn't say which wallet it came from, the message reads **Digital wallet card addition needs your approval** instead.
