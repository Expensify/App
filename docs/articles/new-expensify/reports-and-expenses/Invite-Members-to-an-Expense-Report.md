# Invite Members to an Expense Report

Inviting a member to an expense report adds them to the report so they can follow it and comment on it. Use this when someone outside the approval workflow needs visibility into a report before it is submitted.

An invited member can see every expense on the report, so only invite people who should see all of it. You can invite members while the report is still in **Draft** status. Once the report is submitted, the **Invite member** button no longer appears.

---

## Who can invite members to an expense report

- The member who submitted the report
- Workspace Admins on the report's workspace

You can only invite while the report is in **Draft** status. Inviting is not available on submitted, approved, paid, or archived reports, and it is not available on expenses you submit to an individual person instead of a workspace.

---

## How to invite members to an expense report

1. Open the expense report.
2. Click the report header to open **Details**.
3. Select **Members**.
4. Click **Invite member**.
5. Search for a person by name, email, or phone number, then select them. You can select more than one person.
6. Click **Invite**.

## What happens after you invite a member to an expense report

- The invited person is added to the report's member list.
- They can open the report and see every expense on it, including receipts and amounts.
- They can comment on the report and on individual expenses.
- Being invited to a report does not add the person to your workspace and does not make them an approver. Approval still follows the workspace approval workflow.

# FAQ

## Why can't I see the Invite member button on an expense report?

The button only appears when all of the following are true:

- The report is in **Draft** status.
- You submitted the report, or you are a Workspace Admin on that report's workspace.
- The report is not archived.

If the report has already been submitted, retract it first. [Learn how to retract and edit a submitted report](/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Edit-Reports).

## Can I remove someone I invited to an expense report?

No. The report's **Members** page does not offer a remove option for expense reports.

## Does inviting someone to an expense report let them approve or pay it?

No. Invited members get visibility and can comment, but approval and payment still follow the workspace approval workflow. [Learn more about what each role can do on a report](/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Expense-and-Report-Actions).

## How do I invite someone to a chat room or group chat instead?

Chat rooms and group chats use a separate flow, and any participant of a group chat can invite. [Learn how to invite members to groups or rooms](/articles/new-expensify/chat/Chat-Features-and-Moderation-Tools).
