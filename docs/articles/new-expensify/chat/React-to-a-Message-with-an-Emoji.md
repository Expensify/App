# How to add an emoji reaction to a message in New Expensify

An emoji reaction lets you respond to a chat message without sending a new message. Reactions appear directly under the message with a count, so everyone in the chat can see who reacted and with what. Use a reaction when you want to acknowledge a message, show agreement, or celebrate an update without adding to the conversation.

On web you can add a reaction with your mouse or with your keyboard.

---

## How to add an emoji reaction to a message in New Expensify

**Web:**

1. Open a chat from **Inbox**.
2. Hover over the message you want to react to. A toolbar appears above the message with 👍, ❤️, and 😂, followed by an **Add reaction** button.
3. Click one of the emojis to react with it, or click **Add reaction** and choose any other emoji.

You can also right-click the message to open the full message menu. Its reaction row shows 👍, ❤️, 😂, and 🔥, followed by an **Add reaction** button.

**Mobile:**

1. Tap a chat from **Inbox**.
2. Long-press the message you want to react to.
3. Tap an emoji in the reaction row, or tap **Add reaction** and choose any other emoji.

---

## How to move between emoji reactions using the keyboard in New Expensify

Keyboard navigation of the reaction toolbar is available on web only.

1. Hover over a message to show the reaction toolbar, or right-click the message to open the full message menu.
2. Press **Tab** to highlight the first emoji in the reaction row.
3. Press the **Left arrow** or **Right arrow** key to move the highlight between the emojis. The highlight wraps around, so pressing **Right arrow** on the last emoji moves to the first one, and pressing **Left arrow** on the first emoji moves to the last one.
4. Press **Enter** to react with the highlighted emoji.

The arrow keys only move the highlight after you press **Tab**. If you opened the toolbar and clicked an emoji with your mouse instead of pressing **Tab**, the arrow keys will not move the highlight.

---

## What happens after you add an emoji reaction to a message

- The emoji appears in a reaction bubble under the message, along with the number of people who reacted with it.
- Hover over the reaction bubble to see the names of the people who reacted with that emoji.
- Select the reaction bubble again to remove your reaction. The bubble disappears once nobody is reacting with that emoji.
- Select the **Add reaction** button at the end of the row of reaction bubbles to react with another emoji.

# FAQ

## Can I remove an emoji reaction I already added?

Yes. Select the reaction bubble under the message again to remove your reaction. If you were the only person reacting with that emoji, the bubble is removed from the message.

## Why don't the arrow keys move between emoji reactions?

The arrow keys only move the highlight after you press **Tab** to enter the reaction row. If you clicked an emoji with your mouse, press **Tab** first, then use the **Left arrow** and **Right arrow** keys.

## Can I react with an emoji that isn't in the toolbar?

Yes. Select **Add reaction** and choose any emoji from the emoji picker.

## Can I add an emoji reaction on mobile?

Yes. Long-press the message, then tap an emoji in the reaction row or tap **Add reaction**. Keyboard navigation of the reaction row is available on web only.
