# Admin Rooms in Expensify Chat

Every workspace includes an **#admins** room, and workspaces with invited members also include an **#announce** room. These rooms help teams stay informed and allow Workspace Admins to collaborate behind the scenes.

---

## Who can use the #announce and #admins rooms

- **#announce** is available to all Workspace Members. By default, all Workspace Members can post messages in it, and Workspace Admins can restrict posting to admins only.
- **#admins** is only visible to Workspace Admins.

Your workspace gets an **#announce** room once you invite members to it. A workspace you use on your own has an **#admins** room but no **#announce** room.

---

## What you can do in the #announce room

The **#announce** room is ideal for sharing company-wide updates, policy announcements, or important event reminders.

🔒 **Note:** Anyone in the workspace can read messages in the #announce room, but only Admins can post if posting is restricted to admins.

---

## How to restrict messaging in the #announce room to admins only

1. Open the **#announce** room from your inbox.
2. Click the room header.
3. Click **Settings**.
4. Under **Who can post**, select **Admins only**.
5. Click **Save**.

---

## What you can do in the #admins room

The **#admins** room is designed for secure communication between admins and Expensify support contacts.

**You can use the #admins room to:**
- Coordinate with your internal admin team.
- Chat directly with your **Expensify Account Executive**.
- Communicate with your **Account Manager** (available with subscriptions of 100+ members).
- Track changes made to workspace settings by any admin or Expensify representative.

🔐 **Note:** Only Workspace Admins can view or access this room.

---

## How to open the #admins room when it is not in your Inbox

If you created your workspace by selecting **Track expenses for my business** or **Organize my personal spending** during onboarding, the **#admins** room is not shown in your Inbox chat list. The room still exists, and you can open it from the workspace:

1. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), click **Workspaces > [Workspace name]**.
2. Click **Rooms**.
3. Select **#admins**.
4. Click **Go to room**.

Learn more about [finding rooms in a workspace](/articles/new-expensify/chat/Find-Rooms-in-a-Workspace).

# FAQ

## Why is someone I don’t recognize in my #admins room?

If you're seeing an unfamiliar name in your **#admins** room, it’s likely your **Expensify Account Executive** or **Account Manager**. These team members are added automatically to help you onboard and provide product support.

## Why don’t I see the #admins room in my Inbox?

Workspaces created through the **Track expenses for my business** or **Organize my personal spending** onboarding options don’t show the **#admins** room in the Inbox chat list. Open it from **Workspaces > [Workspace name] > Rooms** instead.

## Why doesn’t my workspace have an #announce room?

The **#announce** room is added once you invite members to your workspace. If you’re the only person on the workspace, you’ll only see the **#admins** room and your workspace chat.
