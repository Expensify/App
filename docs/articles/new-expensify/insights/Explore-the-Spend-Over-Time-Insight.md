# Explore the Spend Over Time Insight

**Spend over time** shows how total expenses change over a selected date range, helping you identify spending trends, increases, and decreases.

You can use **Spend over time** to:

- Monitor overall spending trends.
- Identify unusual increases or decreases in spending.
- Compare spending across days, weeks, months, quarters, or years.
- Spot recurring or seasonal spending patterns.
- Support budgeting and forecasting decisions.

---

## What Spend over time shows

**Spend over time** displays spending trends as a line chart, making it easy to see how spending changes throughout the selected date range.

Each point on the chart represents a time period. You can view:

- **Total spend:** The combined amount of expenses during that period.
- **Percentage of total spend:** The period's share of overall spending.
- **Number of expenses:** How many expenses contribute to the total.

This makes it easier to identify periods with unusually high or low spending and compare spending patterns over time.

## Who can see Spend over time

**Spend over time** is available to all members. 

The expenses included depend on your permissions:

- **Workspace admins and auditors** can see expenses across their workspace.
- **Approvers** can see expenses submitted to them.
- **Members** can see their own expenses.

You won't see expenses you don't have permission to access.

[Learn who can see insights](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Learn-About-Insights#who-can-see-insights).

## How Spend over time is calculated

**Spend over time** groups expenses by time period and calculates the total spending for each period.

Results include expenses within the selected date range that you have permission to view. Amounts are displayed using the selected **Group currency**.

**Spend over time** includes a **Group by** option that lets you change how expenses are grouped along the timeline.

The available options are:

- **Day:** View daily spending changes.
- **Week:** Compare spending across weeks.
- **Month:** Track monthly spending trends.
- **Quarter:** Compare spending across quarters.
- **Year:** Review annual spending trends.

**Month** is selected by default.

To change the grouping:

1. On the **Spend over time** visualization, select **Group by**.
2. Choose **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Quarter**, or **Year**.
3. Select **Apply**.

The chart updates to show spending grouped by your selected time period.

The **Date**, **Workspace**, and **Group currency** filters are shared across all insights, allowing you to adjust which data appears.

[Learn how to filter insights](/articles/new-expensify/insights/How-to-Use-Insights#how-to-filter-insights).

## How to explore Spend over time

**Spend over time** displays spending across the selected date range, grouped by your chosen time period.

To explore the underlying expenses, select **More (⋮)** on the visualization, then select **View on Spend**. This opens the related results in **Spend**, where you can review individual expenses and analyze spending in more detail.

[Learn how to explore expenses from an insight](/articles/new-expensify/insights/How-to-Use-Insights#how-to-explore-expenses-from-an-insight).

# FAQ

## Can you export Spend over time?

Grouped totals and chart summaries can't be exported directly from **Spend over time**, but you can export the underlying expenses from **Spend**.

[Learn how to export expenses](/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/How-to-Export-Expenses).

## What other insights are available?

Additional insights help you analyze spending from different perspectives:

- **Top spenders** shows which members have the highest total spending.
- **Top merchants** shows which merchants account for the most spending.
- **Top categories** shows which expense categories account for the most spending.

[Learn more about the available insights](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Learn-About-Insights#what-insights-are-available).

## Why is an expense missing from Spend over time?

An expense might not appear if:

- You don't have permission to view it.
- It falls outside the selected date range.
- It belongs to a workspace excluded by the **Workspace** filter.

To review the underlying expenses and apply additional filters, select **More (⋮)**, then **View on Spend**.
