# Use AI Insight Cards and Explain

AI insight cards highlight spending patterns worth reviewing on the Spend dashboard in **Insights**. Select **Explain** on a card or chart to discuss its data with Concierge.

## How AI insight cards highlight spending patterns

Rules identify spending spikes, trends, and concentrations in your expense data. AI turns those findings into short summaries. Each card shows a statistic and a summary of the detected pattern.

Cards draw on the Spend dashboard's charts:

- [Spend over time](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Explore-the-Spend-Over-Time-Insight) for spending changes across periods.
- [Top merchants](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Explore-the-Top-Merchants-Insight), [Top categories](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Explore-the-Top-Categories-Insight), and [Top spenders](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Explore-the-Top-Spenders-Insight) for where displayed spending is concentrated.

Cards need about a month of expense history to establish a baseline: at least one expense you can access must be dated 30 or more days ago. Having enough history doesn't guarantee a card will appear; a spending pattern must also meet the detection rules. Charts can appear as soon as matching expense data is available.

## How to use AI insight cards in Insights

1. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), select **Insights**.
2. Adjust **Date**, **Workspace**, and **Group currency** to choose the spending data to review.
3. Review the cards above the charts. On mobile, swipe across the row to see more cards.
4. Select **Explain** on a card to ask Concierge about that finding.

Cards may take time to load after the charts appear. Changing the selected data or the time grouping can change which cards appear.

For shared filter instructions, [learn how to use Insights](/articles/new-expensify/insights/How-to-Use-Insights).

## How to use Explain on an Insights chart

1. In **Insights**, find the Spend chart you want to discuss.
2. Select **Explain** in the chart header.
3. Review the automatically sent question in Concierge, then ask follow-up questions.

**Explain** opens a new Concierge conversation about the selected card or chart, with your date, workspace, and group currency included as context. On web with a wide layout, the conversation opens in a side panel. On mobile, it opens as a full-screen conversation.

# FAQ

## Why don't I see AI insight cards?

Cards need about a month of accessible expense history and a spending pattern that meets the detection rules. They may also still be loading. Seeing charts without cards doesn't mean your expense data is missing.

## Is the AI making up the insight cards?

Rules detect the findings in your own expense data; AI writes the summaries. The cards describe spending patterns, not proven causes. Use **Explain** to discuss a finding with Concierge and review the underlying expenses before acting on it.

## Does Explain include every chart setting?

The selected date, workspace, and group currency are included. **Compare** and the chart's **Group by** selection aren't passed as separate settings. Mention those settings in your follow-up question if they're important to your analysis.
