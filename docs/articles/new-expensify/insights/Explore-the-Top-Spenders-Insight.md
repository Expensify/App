# Explore the Top Spenders Insight

**Top spenders** shows which members submitted the highest total expenses across workspaces where you're a workspace admin, approver, or auditor.

You can use **Top spenders** to:

- Identify unusually high spending.
- Monitor spending patterns by member.
- Spot spending that may need a closer review for policy compliance.
- Support expense approval and reimbursement reviews.

---

## What Top spenders shows

**Top spenders** highlights the five members with the highest total spending, ranked from highest to lowest.

Each member is represented by a bar, making it easy to compare spending amounts. For each member, you can see:

- **Total spend:** The combined amount of their expenses.
- **Number of expenses:** How many expenses contribute to their total.

This makes it easier to compare spending across members and identify expenses that may need further review.

## Who can see Top spenders

**Top spenders** is available to workspace admins, auditors, and approvers on workspaces with at least two members.

The expenses included depend on your permissions:

- **Workspace admins and auditors** can see expenses across their workspace.
- **Approvers** can see expenses submitted to them.

Members without these permissions won't see **Top spenders**.

[Learn who can see insights](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Learn-About-Insights#who-can-see-insights).

---

## How Top spenders is calculated 

**Top spenders** groups expenses by the member who submitted them and ranks members from highest to lowest total spend.

Results include expenses within the selected date range from workspaces where you have the required permissions. Expenses submitted in individual chats instead of to a workspace aren't included.

The **Date**, **Workspace**, and **Group currency** filters are shared across all insights, allowing you to adjust which data appears.

[Learn how to filter insights](/articles/new-expensify/insights/How-to-Use-Insights#how-to-filter-insights). 

---

## How to explore Top spenders

**Top spenders** displays the five members with the highest total spend for the selected date range.

To see spend for every member, select **More (⋮)** on the visualization, then select **View on Spend**. This opens the related results in **Spend**, where you can explore spend beyond the top five members.

[Learn how to explore expenses from an insight](/articles/new-expensify/insights/How-to-Use-Insights#how-to-explore-expenses-from-an-insight).

# FAQ

## Can you export Top spenders?

**Top spenders** totals can't be exported directly. However, if you expand each group to reveal the individual expenses, you can then select those expenses and use Export to CSV to download the raw data. 

[Learn how to export expenses](/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/How-to-Export-Expenses).

## What other insights are available?

Additional insights help you analyze spending from different perspectives:

- **Spend over time** shows how total expenses change over a date range.
- **Top merchants** shows which vendors received the most payments.
- **Top categories** shows the highest-spending expense types (e.g., Travel, Meals).

[Learn more about the available insights](/articles/new-expensify/insights/Learn-About-Insights#what-insights-are-available)

## Why is an expense missing from Top spenders?

An expense might not appear if:
- It belongs to a workspace you don't have permission to view.
- It falls outside the selected date range.
- It was submitted in an individual chat rather than to a workspace.
- The member isn't among the five highest spenders shown in the visualization.

To see spend for members outside the top five, select **More (⋮)**, then **View on Spend**.
