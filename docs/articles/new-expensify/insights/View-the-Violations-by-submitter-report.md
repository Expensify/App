# View the Violations by submitter report

The Violations by submitter report shows which members submitted the most expenses with violations last month. Use it to identify repeated violations, spot compliance patterns, and decide where guidance or follow-up may be needed.

The report is a pre-built suggested search and is available only for Control workspaces with Rules enabled.

If Rules are not enabled, [learn how to set up Workspace Rules](/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules).

---

## Who can use the Violations by submitter report

Workspace Admins and Auditors can use the Violations by submitter report on web and mobile when:

 - The workspace is on the Control plan.
 - Rules are enabled for the workspace.
 - The workspace has two or more members.

---

## How to view the Violations by submitter report

**Web:**  
1. Use the navigation tabs on the left and select **Spend**. 
2. Under **Insights**, select **Violations by submitter**.

**Mobile:**  
1. From the navigation tabs on the bottom, tap **Spend**.
2. Tap the hamburger menu in the top-left corner, then select **Violations by submitter**.

The report groups last month’s expenses that had a violation when submitted by the member who submitted them.

---

## What information the Violations by submitter report displays

The Violations by submitter report groups expenses that had a violation when submitted by the member who submitted them.

Each row represents a member and shows:

 - **From** – the member who submitted the expenses
 - **Expenses** – the number of expenses with violations the member submitted
 - **Total** – the total amount of those expenses

Select a row to view the member’s individual expenses. The **Violations** column shows the violations associated with each expense, such as a missing receipt or an amount over a workspace limit. If an expense has multiple violations, they are separated by commas.

# FAQ

## Can I customize the Violations by submitter report?

You can change filters such as the date range, workspace, or member to explore the results further. Because Violations by submitter is a built-in suggested search, you can’t save changes directly to the suggested search.

To create your own custom search, [learn how to use search operators to filter and analyze spend](/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Use-Search-Operators-to-Filter-and-Analyze).

## Can I export the Violations by submitter report?

Not directly — the Violations by submitter report can’t be exported with its grouped totals or summary data. However, if you expand each group to reveal the individual expenses, you can then select those expenses and use Export to CSV to download the raw data.

## How is the Violations by submitter report calculated?

The report uses expenses from the previous calendar month that had a violation at the time they were submitted, groups them by the member who submitted them, and shows the top 10 members by the number of expenses with violations.
