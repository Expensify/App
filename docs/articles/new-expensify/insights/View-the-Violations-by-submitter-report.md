# View the Violations by submitter report

The Violations by submitter report shows which members submitted the most expenses that broke your workspace rules last month. It’s a fast way to:

- See who is repeatedly submitting expenses with policy violations
- Spot compliance patterns without reviewing reports one by one
- Decide where to focus policy guidance or follow-up

This report is a pre-built suggested search, so you can review compliance activity at a glance. It relies on Rules, so it’s only available on workspaces that have Rules enabled.

If Rules are not enabled on your workspace, [learn how to set up Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules).

---

## Who can use the Violations by submitter report

The Violations by submitter report is available to Workspace Admins and Auditors on both web and mobile. It appears only on workspaces on the Control plan that have Rules enabled.

**Note:** Exporting data is only available on web.

---

## Where to find the Violations by submitter report

**Web:**  
Use the navigation tabs on the left and select **Spend**, then click **Violations by submitter** under the **Insights** section.

**Mobile:**  
Tap **Spend** from the navigation tabs on the bottom, then tap the hamburger menu in the top-right corner. Under **Insights**, tap **Violations by submitter**.

---

## What information the Violations by submitter report displays

The Violations by submitter report is powered by a saved search query using Expensify's grouping and filtering engine. It groups last month’s expenses that had a violation at submission by the member who submitted them. The report shows:

- **From** – the member who submitted the expenses
- **Expenses** – the number of expenses with a violation each member submitted
- **Total** – the total amount of those expenses

Results are sorted by the number of expenses with violations, from highest to lowest.

---

## How to interpret the Violations by submitter report

Each row represents an individual member, sorted in descending order by the number of expenses with violations. Click any row to expand it and view that member’s individual expenses.

When you expand a row, a **Violations** column lists the specific violations on each expense (for example, a missing receipt or an amount over limit). If an expense has more than one violation, they’re shown separated by commas.

## Can you customize the Violations by submitter report?

Yes - you can adjust filters like date range, workspace, or member to explore compliance trends. However, the Violations by submitter report is a built-in suggested search, so you can’t save changes to it directly.

To create and save a custom report:

1. Go to the **Spend** or **Expenses** tab.
2. Use filters to adjust grouping and timeframes.
3. Apply filters and save your custom search.

[Learn how to use search operators to filter and analyze spend](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Use-Search-Operators-to-Filter-and-Analyze).

## Ways to use the Violations by submitter report

Workspace Admins and finance teams use the Violations by submitter report to:

- Identify who is repeatedly submitting expenses that break the rules
- Prioritize coaching or policy reminders for specific members
- Support compliance and audit reviews without exporting data

---

## How to switch views in the Violations by submitter report

By default, the Violations by submitter report displays a **Table**. You can also switch to **Bar**, **Line**, or **Pie** view depending on how you want to analyze your data.

- **Bar** shows totals for easy comparison.
- **Line** shows trends over time.
- **Pie** shows how data is distributed.

To change the view:

1. Open the Violations by submitter report.
2. Click **View** in the top navigation.
3. Select **Bar**, **Line**, or **Pie**.

The report will update instantly to reflect your selected view.

# FAQ

## Can you export the Violations by submitter report?

Not directly — the Violations by submitter report can’t be exported with its grouped totals or summary data. However, if you expand each group to reveal the individual expenses, you can then select those expenses and use Export to CSV to download the raw data.

1. Go to the **Spend** tab.
2. Apply filters to create your own view.
3. Click **Export to CSV**.

## Why can’t I see the Violations by submitter report?

The Violations by submitter report only appears when both of the following are true:

- You’re a Workspace Admin or Auditor on the workspace.
- The workspace is on the Control plan and has Rules enabled.

If Rules aren’t enabled, the report won’t appear. [Learn how to set up Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules).

## How is the Violations by submitter report calculated?

The report uses expenses from the previous calendar month that had a violation at the time they were submitted, groups them by the member who submitted them, and shows the top 10 members by the number of expenses with violations.

## What other Insights reports are available?

Additional Insights reports help you analyze spending from different perspectives:

- **Top Spenders** shows which members submitted the highest total expenses.
- **Top Merchants** shows which vendors received the most payments.
- **Top Categories** shows the highest-spending expense types (e.g., Travel, Meals).
