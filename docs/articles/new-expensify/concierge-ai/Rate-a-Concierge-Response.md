# How to rate a Concierge response in Expensify

When Concierge answers you in chat, you can rate the answer with a thumbs up or a thumbs down. Your rating tells Expensify which Concierge answers are helping and which ones need work, and a thumbs down lets Concierge follow up on what went wrong.

Rating a response takes one click and does not change or delete the answer.

---

## Who can rate a Concierge response

All Expensify members can rate a Concierge response. No special permissions or plan are required.

You can rate Concierge responses anywhere you chat with Concierge.

[Learn where to find Concierge](/articles/new-expensify/concierge-ai/Concierge-Basics#where-to-find-concierge).

---

## How to rate a Concierge response

1. Open your chat with Concierge and send a question.
2. Wait for Concierge to finish responding.
3. Below the response, find **Was that response useful?**
4. Click 👍 if the response helped you, or 👎 if it did not.

On web, hovering over each option shows its label: **Useful response** for 👍 and **Not useful response** for 👎.

## What happens after you rate a Concierge response

- Your rating is added to the response as a 👍 or 👎 emoji reaction, the same way reactions work on any other chat message.
- **Thanks for the feedback!** replaces the prompt for a few seconds, then disappears.
- The prompt does not come back on that response while your reaction is there, including after you reload Expensify.
- If you rate a response **Not useful response**, Concierge may reply in a thread on that response to ask what was wrong.

---

## How to change or remove your rating of a Concierge response

Your rating behaves like any other emoji reaction, so you can undo it:

1. Find the 👍 or 👎 reaction below the Concierge response you rated.
2. Click the reaction to remove it.

Once your reaction is removed, **Was that response useful?** becomes available on that response again if it is still the most recent Concierge response, and you can rate it differently.

# FAQ

## Which Concierge responses can I rate?

Only the most recent Concierge response in the chat. When Concierge answers again, the prompt moves to the new response, and any response you already rated keeps its reaction.

## Can other people see how I rated a Concierge response?

Your rating is stored as an emoji reaction on the response, so anyone who can see that chat can see the reaction.

## Why don't I see the prompt under a Concierge response?

The prompt only appears under the most recent finished Concierge response. You won't see it if you already rated that response, if Concierge is still writing its answer, or if the message is a greeting rather than an answer to a question.

## Do I have to rate a Concierge response?

No. Rating is optional, and skipping it doesn't affect the answer or your ability to keep chatting with Concierge.

## How do I get more help after rating a response as not useful?

Reply in the chat with what you were expecting, or ask to talk to a human. Concierge connects you with an Expensify team member 24/7.
