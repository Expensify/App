When you send Concierge a question on web, you don't have to sit and watch the chat. Expensify changes your browser tab so you can tell at a glance that a reply is on the way or waiting for you, then switches the tab back once you've read it.

This feature is not available on mobile.

## What the Concierge browser tab indicator shows

While Concierge is working on a question you sent, and until you read the reply, your browser tab changes in two ways:

- The tab icon changes to the Concierge icon with a red dot.
- The tab title changes to **Concierge has an answer!**

If you have unread messages elsewhere in Expensify, the unread count stays in front of the tab title, so the tab reads **(3) Concierge has an answer!** rather than hiding how many unread messages you have.

## How to see when Concierge has an answer while you work in another tab

1. Open the Concierge chat, or type your question in the search bar and select **Ask Concierge**.
2. Send your question.
3. Switch to another browser tab or another chat and keep working.
4. Watch your Expensify browser tab for the Concierge icon and the **Concierge has an answer!** title.
5. Return to the chat to read the reply.

## When the Concierge browser tab indicator appears

The indicator only appears for replies to questions you sent yourself. It covers questions you send:

- In the Concierge chat
- In a reply thread on a Concierge message
- In the #admins room of a workspace

Messages Concierge or anyone else posts without you asking a question first do not change your browser tab. In the #admins room, this means ordinary chat messages leave the tab icon alone.

## When the Concierge browser tab indicator clears

The browser tab returns to the ordinary Expensify icon and page title when any of the following happens:

- You read the reply.
- Your question fails to send.
- Concierge doesn't respond within two minutes.
- You reload Expensify, which restores the ordinary unread icon.

# FAQ

## Why doesn't my browser tab change when I ask Concierge a question?

Check that you are using Expensify on web in a browser tab, that you sent the question yourself, and that you have switched away from the reply. The indicator is only shown on web, only for questions you sent, and it clears as soon as you view the reply.

## Does the Concierge browser tab indicator change my unread count?

No. The unread count shown in the tab title and in the app is unchanged. The indicator replaces the tab icon and title text only.

## Can I get the Concierge browser tab indicator on the mobile app?

No. This feature is not available on mobile. On mobile, keep using your ordinary Expensify notifications to see when Concierge replies.
