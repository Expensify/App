# How to Pay Expenses in New Expensify

Paying expenses in New Expensify is fast and easy—whether you're reimbursing a teammate through a Workspace or sending money to a friend personally. You can pay using a connected business bank account or your Wallet.

Every time you pay, Expensify asks you to choose a payment method. Selecting **Pay** always opens the payment method menu, even if you have paid before, so nothing is sent—or marked as paid—until you pick a method.

---

## What you need before you pay Workspace expenses

To pay Workspace expenses, you’ll first need to:
- [Connect a business bank account](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/expenses-and-payments/Connect-a-Business-Bank-Account)
- Ask the submitter to [connect a personal bank account](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/expenses-and-payments/Connect-a-Personal-Bank-Account)

---

## Where to find Workspace expenses to pay

- On web, go to the **navigation tabs on the left** and select **Spend > Reports > To-do > Pay**  
- On mobile, go to the **navigation tabs on the bottom** and select **Spend > Reports > To-do > Pay**  
- Or, select the notification you received via email, push, or text

---

## How to pay Workspace expenses

1. Open the expense report from **Spend > Reports** or from the notification.
2. Select **Pay**. The button includes the report total, for example **Pay $14.62**.
3. Select a payment method from the menu that opens:
   - **Pay with business account** to send the payment from your connected business bank account
   - **Mark as paid** to record a payment you made outside Expensify

**Note:** Only bank accounts that match the report's currency will appear as payment options. For example, if a report is in EUR, only EUR bank accounts are shown.

## What you need before you pay personal expenses

To send a personal payment, you must first [set up your Wallet](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/expenses-and-payments/Set-up-your-wallet).  
**Note:** Personal payments are currently available only to U.S. customers.

---

## Where to find personal expenses to pay

- On web, go to the **navigation tabs on the left** and select **Spend > Reports > To-do > Pay**  
- On mobile, go to the **navigation tabs on the bottom** and select **Spend > Reports > To-do > Pay**  
- Or, select the notification you received

---

## How to pay a personal expense

1. Open the personal expense from your notification or **Spend > Reports**.
2. Select **Pay**. The button includes the amount, for example **Pay $14.62**.
3. Select a payment method from the menu that opens:
   - **Pay with wallet** to pay from your Wallet (personal bank account)
   - **Pay with business account** to pay from a connected Workspace business bank account
   - **Mark as paid** to record a payment you made outside Expensify

# FAQ

## How long does it take for the submitter to receive a payment?

For US-based ACH payments, if the payment is **under $100** and your total withdrawals in the past 24 hours are **under $10,000**, the payment will process in **one business day**.

Otherwise, standard bank transfer times apply, and the recipient will receive their funds within **4–5 business days**.

---

## Why doesn't the Pay button use the payment method I chose last time?

Expensify no longer pre-selects your most recent payment method, and the **Pay** button no longer has a separate dropdown arrow. Selecting **Pay** opens the payment method menu every time so that a payment is never sent, or marked as paid, without you choosing how.

---

## Can I pay someone in another currency?

You can reimburse Workspace expenses using business bank accounts in the following currencies:
- United States (USD)
- Canada (CAD)
- United Kingdom (GBP)
- European Union (EUR)
- Australia (AUD)

**Note:** Personal expenses paid with the Wallet can only be paid in **USD**.

---

## Why don't I see my bank account when I select Pay?

Only bank accounts that match the report's currency appear as payment options. If you have a USD bank account but the report is in EUR, the USD account will not be shown. To pay the report, connect a bank account in the matching currency, or select **Mark as paid** to record a payment made outside Expensify.

---

## How do I pay a report with only non-reimbursable expenses?

Reports containing only non-reimbursable expenses (e.g., company card charges) cannot be paid via ACH through Expensify. Instead, select **Pay**, then select **Mark as paid** from the payment method menu to close the report.
