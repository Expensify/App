# Delegate When Out of Office

If you're out of the office or on vacation, you can assign a **Vacation delegate** to handle your approvals while you're away.

A **Vacation delegate** can approve reports on your behalf. Once assigned, any reports sent to you for approval will be automatically redirected to your delegate. When you're back, just remove the delegate to resume handling your own reports.

All delegate actions are tracked in the report history for full visibility.

If someone needs ongoing access to manage expenses on your behalf instead of temporary approval coverage, [learn how to add them as a Copilot](/articles/new-expensify/settings/Manage-Copilot-Access).

## Who can assign a Vacation delegate

Any member can assign their own **Vacation delegate**. Only you can assign your delegate — a Workspace Admin can't assign one for you.

## How to assign a Vacation delegate

1. Go to **Account > Profile > Status**.
2. Select **Vacation delegate**.
3. In the **Name, email, or phone number** field, search for the person you want as your delegate.
4. Select them from the list.

Your delegate is saved as soon as you select them, and you don't need to click **Save**. Any reports or chats that would normally come to you will be redirected to your delegate, and you can view every action they take in the report's history and comments.

If the person you select isn't a member of every workspace your approvals come from, an extra step appears before the delegate is saved. See the next section.

## How to invite a Vacation delegate who is missing from a workspace

When the person you select isn't a member of every workspace your approvals come from, the **Vacation delegate** page lists the workspaces they're missing from and asks what to do before saving the delegate. The workspaces are grouped under one or both of these headings:

- **You are an admin of these workspaces:** workspaces you administer, so you can add your delegate to them yourself.
- **You are a member of these workspaces:** workspaces you don't administer. Those Workspace Admins will be asked to add your delegate.

What you do next depends on which groups are listed:

- If only workspaces you administer are listed, click **Invite** to add your delegate to each one and save the delegate, or click **Skip** to save the delegate without adding them to any workspace.
- If only workspaces you don't administer are listed, click **Confirm** to save the delegate. Those Workspace Admins will be asked to add them.
- If both groups are listed, click **Invite** to add your delegate to the workspaces you administer and save the delegate, or click **Skip** to save the delegate without adding them to any workspace. Either way, the admins of the workspaces you don't administer will be asked to add them.

If you leave this page without clicking **Invite**, **Skip**, or **Confirm**, your delegate isn't changed and stays as it was before.

## How to remove your Vacation delegate

When you're ready to take back control of your reports:

1. Go to **Account > Profile > Status**.
2. Select the **Vacation delegate** row showing your delegate's name.
3. Select your delegate's name again to remove them.

Your delegate is removed as soon as you select them again. There's no confirmation prompt, and you don't need to click **Save**.

# FAQ

## Can my admin assign a Vacation delegate for me?

No. In New Expensify, only you can assign your own **Vacation delegate**.

## Why can't my Vacation delegate reimburse reports?

Your delegate can only reimburse reports if they also have access to the reimbursement account in your workspace. Without access, they won't see the option to reimburse—even if they're your delegate.

## What if my delegate is also on vacation?

Your delegate can assign their own **Vacation delegate** too. This ensures approvals keep moving, even when multiple members are away.

## Does clearing my status remove my Vacation delegate?

No. Clearing your status won't affect your delegate. You'll need to manually remove them when you're back.

## What happens if my delegate isn't a member of my workspaces?

You'll see the missing workspaces listed before your delegate is saved. You can invite them yourself to any workspace you administer, and the admins of the workspaces you don't administer will be asked to add them.

## Does skipping the invite still save my delegate?

Yes. Clicking **Skip** saves your delegate without adding them to any workspace.

## What should I use if someone needs to manage my expenses permanently?

A **Vacation delegate** is intended for temporary approval coverage while you're away. If someone needs ongoing access to manage expenses on your behalf—such as an executive assistant, accountant, or bookkeeper, [add them as a Copilot](/articles/new-expensify/settings/Manage-Copilot-Access) instead.

Copilots provide long-term account access for expense management, while Vacation delegates are designed for temporary approval coverage.
