# BC68 Sync Error in Dynamics 365 Business Central

If the **Accounting** page or a report shows this error:

> BC68 Sync Error: Could not read the stored Business Central configuration.

Expensify couldn't read the Business Central settings saved for your workspace, such as your import and export settings. Expensify can't sync or export reports until Concierge fixes them.

## Why the BC68 sync error happens

The BC68 error is a problem with the settings saved in Expensify. It isn't caused by Business Central.

## How to resolve the BC68 sync error

Contact Concierge and include the name of the workspace.

## When to contact Concierge

Contact Concierge as soon as you see the BC68 error.
