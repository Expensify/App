# BC53 Sync Error in Dynamics 365 Business Central

If the **Accounting** page or a report shows this error:

> BC53 Sync Error: Could not build the Business Central request.

Expensify couldn't prepare a request to send to Business Central, so it didn't send anything. This error can appear on the **Accounting** page after a sync, or in a report's history after an export.

## Why the BC53 sync error happens

The BC53 error is a problem on Expensify's side. You can't fix it in Business Central or in your workspace settings.

## How to resolve the BC53 sync error

Contact Concierge. Include the name of the workspace, and the report's name if the error appeared on a report.

## When to contact Concierge

Contact Concierge as soon as you see the BC53 error. Retrying the sync or export is unlikely to help.
