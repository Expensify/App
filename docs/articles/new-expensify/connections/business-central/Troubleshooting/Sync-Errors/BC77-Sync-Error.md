# BC77 Sync Error in Dynamics 365 Business Central

If the **Accounting** page or a report shows this error:

> BC77 Sync Error: Could not read the stored Business Central data.

Expensify couldn't read the Business Central data saved for your workspace, such as the vendors and bank accounts from the last sync. Expensify can't sync or export reports until Concierge fixes it.

## Why the BC77 sync error happens

The BC77 error is a problem with the data saved in Expensify. It isn't caused by Business Central.

## How to fix the BC77 sync error in Business Central

Contact Concierge and include the name of the workspace.

## When to contact Concierge

Contact Concierge as soon as you see the BC77 error.
