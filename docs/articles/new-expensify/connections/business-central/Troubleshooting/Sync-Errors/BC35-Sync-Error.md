# BC35 Sync Error in Dynamics 365 Business Central

If the **Accounting** page shows this error:

> Can't connect to Dynamics 365 Business Central ("BC35 Sync Error: Could not sync the Business Central connection.")

The sync stopped because of an unexpected problem in Expensify.

## Why the BC35 sync error happens

The BC35 error happens when a sync is interrupted before it finishes, and no other error explains why.

## How to fix the BC35 sync error in Business Central

1. In the navigation tabs (on the left on web, on the bottom on mobile), select **Workspaces > [workspace name] > Accounting**.
2. Select **More (⋮)** next to **Dynamics 365 Business Central**, then select **Sync now**.

Most BC35 errors don't happen again on the next sync.

## When to contact Concierge

Contact Concierge if the BC35 error appears again after you sync.
