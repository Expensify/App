# American Airlines

Connect your American Airlines AAdvantage Business℠ account to Expensify to automatically sync eligible flight receipts.

Once connected, eligible flight receipts automatically sync to Expensify for travelers who have an Expensify account with the same email address as their AAdvantage Business account. Your company continues earning AAdvantage miles as usual.

## Who can connect American Airlines to Expensify

To set up the connection, you must be a Travel Manager for your organization’s AAdvantage Business account.

## How to connect American Airlines to Expensify

1. Sign in to your **AAdvantage Business** account.
2. Go to **Settings > Integrations**.
3. Enable **Expensify**.

## What happens after you connect American Airlines to Expensify

Once the integration is enabled, eligible flight receipts automatically sync to Expensify for travelers who use the same email address for Expensify and their AAdvantage Business account. The receipts appear as expenses in each traveler’s Expensify account.

# FAQ

## Will my past American Airlines receipts sync to Expensify?

No. Only eligible AAdvantage Business ticket purchases made after the integration is enabled will automatically sync to Expensify.

## Can individual travelers turn off receipt syncing?

No. Once a Travel Manager enables the integration, individual travelers can’t turn off receipt syncing.

## Will receipts for seat upgrades, in-flight purchases, or other non-ticket purchases sync to Expensify?

No. Currently, only eligible AAdvantage Business ticket purchase receipts automatically sync to Expensify.

## How are American Airlines receipts matched to people in Expensify?

American Airlines uses the traveler’s email address to match their AAdvantage Business account with their Expensify account. The same email address must be used for both accounts.
