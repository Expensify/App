# Connect to Intuit Enterprise Suite

Intuit Enterprise Suite is a separate connection option on your workspace's **Accounting** page, listed directly below **QuickBooks Online**. Connecting it syncs your accounting data between Intuit Enterprise Suite and Expensify so you can code and export expenses without manual entry.

Intuit Enterprise Suite and QuickBooks Online are separate connections. A workspace can use only one accounting connection at a time.

---

## Who can connect Intuit Enterprise Suite

To connect Intuit Enterprise Suite, you must:

- Be a **Workspace Admin**
- Be on the **Control** plan
- Have an Intuit Enterprise Suite account

The **Intuit Enterprise Suite** option appears on every workspace's **Accounting** page, but clicking **Connect** on a **Collect** plan workspace opens an upgrade message instead of starting the connection. [Learn more about the Control plan](/articles/new-expensify/billing-and-subscriptions/explore-plans-subscriptions-and-pricing/Compare-Collect-and-Control-Plans).

If **Accounting** is not enabled on your workspace, you must turn it on before the **Accounting** menu item appears.

---

## How to connect Intuit Enterprise Suite to your workspace

1. From the navigation tabs (on the left on web, and at the bottom on mobile), go to **Workspaces**
2. Select your workspace
3. Click **More features**
4. Scroll to **Integrate** and enable the **Accounting** toggle
5. Click **Accounting** in the left-hand menu
6. Click **Connect** next to **Intuit Enterprise Suite**
7. Log in with your Intuit credentials when prompted

## What happens after you connect Intuit Enterprise Suite

- Expensify imports your Intuit Enterprise Suite settings into your workspace
- The **Accounting** page shows **Intuit Enterprise Suite** as your connected integration
- You can then set your import, export, and advanced settings

Learn how to [configure your import, export, and advanced settings](/articles/new-expensify/connections/quickbooks-online/Configure-Quickbooks-Online).

# FAQ

## Why do I see an upgrade message when I click Connect for Intuit Enterprise Suite?

The Intuit Enterprise Suite integration is only available on the **Control** plan. On a **Collect** plan workspace, clicking **Connect** opens an upgrade message with an **Upgrade** button. Upgrade the workspace to **Control**, then connect again.

## Do I need a beta or an invite to use Intuit Enterprise Suite?

No. Intuit Enterprise Suite is available to all workspaces, so the option is listed on the **Accounting** page for everyone.

## Can I connect both QuickBooks Online and Intuit Enterprise Suite to the same workspace?

No. A workspace can have only one accounting connection at a time. If QuickBooks Online is already connected, you are prompted to disconnect it before Intuit Enterprise Suite connects.

## Why can't I see Intuit Enterprise Suite on my workspace?

Check the following:

- You are a **Workspace Admin**
- The **Accounting** toggle is enabled under **More features > Integrate**
- You are viewing the **Accounting** page for the correct workspace

## Should I connect QuickBooks Online instead?

Connect **QuickBooks Online** if your Intuit subscription is Simple Start, Essentials, or Essentials Plus. Learn how to [connect to QuickBooks Online](/articles/new-expensify/connections/quickbooks-online/Connect-to-QuickBooks-Online).
