# United Airlines

Send your United Airlines flight receipts directly to Expensify when booking through [united.com](https://www.united.com).

If your company is enrolled in United for Business, you can select **Send to Expensify** when booking a flight to send your receipt directly to your Expensify account. Your United MileagePlus email address must match the email address on your Expensify account.

## Who can send United Airlines receipts to Expensify

To use this integration, you must:

- Be an employee of a company enrolled in United for Business.
- Have a United MileagePlus account with the same email address as your Expensify account.

If your company isn't enrolled in United for Business, contact your United account manager or email [ucdhelp@united.com](mailto:ucdhelp@united.com) to learn more about the enrollment process.

## How to send United Airlines receipts to Expensify

1. Go to [united.com](https://www.united.com) and sign in to your United MileagePlus account.
2. Book your flight.
3. Select **Send to Expensify** when the option appears after booking.
4. Your receipt is sent directly to your Expensify account.

## What happens after you send a United Airlines receipt to Expensify

When you select **Send to Expensify**, United Airlines sends your flight receipt directly to the Expensify account associated with your matching email address. The receipt appears as an expense in your account.

# FAQ

## Does my company need to enroll in United for Business?

Yes. Your company must be enrolled in United for Business to use this integration. Contact your United account manager or [ucdhelp@united.com](mailto:ucdhelp@united.com) for enrollment information.

## Do my United MileagePlus and Expensify email addresses need to match?

Yes. The email address associated with your United MileagePlus account must match the email address on your Expensify account.

## Will my United Airlines receipts automatically sync to Expensify?

No. You must select **Send to Expensify** when booking on [united.com](https://www.united.com) to send your receipt directly to Expensify.
